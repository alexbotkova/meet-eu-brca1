Structure and data information

The viewer maps 6,388 single-substitution records, including all 4,606 prediction targets. The 75 records without a single-substitution mapping remain in the frozen source. Classifications are from that snapshot, not new predictions. A position can have multiple substitutions and classifications. Gray can mean no matching records or a filtered-out position. Colored overlap dots are display markers, not variant structures.

Low-confidence AlphaFold regions and the relative positions of distant regions should be interpreted cautiously. This viewer is for research and is not a clinical interpretation tool.
